Foundation Work Checklist (G+2 Civil)

  • Site marking & layout as per drawings (center lines, grid)
  • Excavation to required depth; check bearing capacity / soil
  • Anti-termite treatment
  • PCC base (plain cement concrete) leveled
  • Reinforcement with proper cover blocks (50mm typical per IS 456)
  • Formwork checked for alignment, plumb & support
  • Pre-pour inspection (rebar, cover, cleanliness)
  • Concreting with proper mix, vibration, no cold joints
  • Curing for 7–14 days (keep moist)
  • Red flags: uneven excavation, poor cover, dirty rebar, no curing

Sewer & Drainage Checklist (per Plumbing Drawing)

Soil/waste pipes: 110mm / 75mm / 40mm PVC. Vents min 7 ft above roof. Starting depth ~350mm from FFL. Slopes: 1:100 at penetrations, 1:200 underground.

  • Floor traps (FT) correctly placed & sealed
  • Vent pipes properly sized, connected & extending ≥7 ft above roof
  • Underground slope maintained (no reverse slope or sags)
  • House trap + manholes (800×600mm) accessible & sealed
  • Gully traps / silt traps where shown
  • Rainwater completely separate from sewer
  • Joints properly solvent-welded or rubber-ringed; no O-rings removed
  • Water/ball test before backfill
  • Cleanouts / rodding eyes at strategic points
  • Red flags: missing vents, wrong slope, mixed rainwater, poor joints

5 Non-Negotiable Rules Before Tiling Bathrooms

From site practice (e.g. design diaries reel):

  1. Plumbing Line-Out: Mark exact fixture centerlines & heights on raw walls before cutting grooves.
  2. Manifold System: Dedicated supply lines from a central hub to avoid pressure drops.
  3. Concealed Cistern Frame: Anchor and level in-wall flush frames securely into slab & wall.
  4. Floor Drainage Slope: Confirm gravity slopes on pipes for rapid discharge.
  5. Hydro-Pressure Test: Hold 7–10 bar for 24 hours to catch micro-leaks before tiling.

Common Mistakes Indian Plumbers Make (Avoid These)

  • Wrong slope on SWR pipes (too flat = solids settle; too steep = water leaves solids behind). Aim ~1:40 to 1:100.
  • Missing or inadequate vents → trap seal loss, foul smell, gurgling.
  • Poor jointing (dirty surfaces, insufficient solvent, removed O-rings, sharp 90° elbows instead of sweeps/Y).
  • No pressure test or water test before concealment/plaster/tile.
  • Mixing rainwater with sewer lines.
  • No traps or dry traps; using S-traps where P-traps are better.
  • Inadequate cleanouts / manholes for future access.
  • Using wrong pipe material (PVC for hot/pressurized lines).
  • Not coordinating fixture positions before finalizing points.
